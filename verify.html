The seller locked in a secret seed and published its SHA-256 fingerprint before the break started. Once the seed is revealed, this page replays the whole run in your own browser — every random draw, in order, from the seed. Nothing here trusts the server's stored answer.

Fingerprint: SHA-256 of the seed. Draw #N: HMAC-SHA256(seed, "publicSeed|N|counter") as the randomness, picking from whatever was still in the row at that point, rejection sampled so every remaining card is equally likely.
